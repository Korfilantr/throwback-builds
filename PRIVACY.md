# Throwback privacy policy

*Effective 7 November 2026*

Throwback shows random photos and videos from your Snapchat chats, from a data export you download from Snapchat yourself. It works entirely on your iPhone. **It has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and it never uploads anything.**

## What Throwback reads

When you pick your Snapchat "My Data" export in the app, Throwback reads:

- the chat photos and videos in it (Snapchat only exports media someone saved in a chat),
- the chat history, only to work out which chat each photo or video belongs to, who sent it and when,
- your friends list, only for display names.

## Where it goes

- Everything Throwback imports is copied into the app's own storage on your iPhone, and stays there.
- Nothing is sent to the developer or anyone else. The app makes no network connections for your data.
- The optional storage saver compresses clips on your iPhone.
- Your clips are **not** in your iCloud backup unless you turn on "Include in iCloud backup" in Settings. If you do, Apple stores them as part of your backup under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
- A clip leaves your iPhone only when you share it yourself with the Share button, to wherever you choose.
- If you turn on "Offer to delete the export files", Throwback deletes the export zips only after you confirm.

## Diagnostics log

Settings → Developer keeps a log on your iPhone to help fix problems: what you did in a session (next, back, pause, like…) and problems the app noticed (a video that didn't show, a clip that couldn't play). It identifies clips by short IDs and file names. It never contains names, chat titles or the content of chats. It leaves your iPhone only if you share it, for example with **Report a problem**.

## Other people's content

Your export includes photos and videos your friends sent in chats. Throwback keeps them on your iPhone only. Please be thoughtful about what you share.

## Deleting your data

Settings → Delete everything removes all clips from Throwback. Deleting the app removes everything, including the diagnostics log. Your Snapchat export files aren't affected either way.

## Children

Throwback is meant for people old enough to use Snapchat (13 and over).

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.
