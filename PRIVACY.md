# Throwback privacy policy

*Effective 8 November 2026*

Throwback shows random photos and videos from your Snapchat chats, from a data export you download from Snapchat yourself. It works entirely on your iPhone. **It has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and it never uploads your photos, videos or data.** (The only thing that can ever go to Apple is described under the Your data tab below, and only if you choose it.)

## What Throwback reads

When you pick your Snapchat "My Data" export in the app, Throwback reads:

- the chat photos and videos in it (Snapchat only exports media someone saved in a chat),
- the chat history, only to work out which chat each photo or video belongs to, who sent it and when,
- your friends list, only for display names.

## Where it goes

- Everything Throwback imports is copied into the app's own storage on your iPhone, and stays there.
- Nothing is sent to the developer or anyone else. The only exceptions are the two Apple Maps uses in the Your data tab, described below.
- The optional storage saver compresses clips on your iPhone.
- Your clips are **not** in your iCloud backup unless you turn on "Include in iCloud backup" in Settings. If you do, Apple stores them as part of your backup under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
- A clip leaves your iPhone only when you share it yourself with the Share button, to wherever you choose.
- If you turn on "Offer to delete the export files", Throwback deletes the export zips only after you confirm.

## Your data tab (off unless you turn it on)

If you turn on the Your data tab in Settings, Throwback also keeps the "about you" files from your export: location history, profile, account and similar files (never chats or snaps). It uses them to show where Snapchat thinks home and work are, your location history day by day, the places you go most, and what Snapchat lists about you. These files are stored in the app on your iPhone, follow the same iCloud backup setting as your clips, and can be deleted at any time from the tab or from Settings.

- **Maps** are drawn by Apple Maps. To draw a map, your iPhone downloads map images for the area on screen from Apple, as in any app that shows a map. Your location history itself isn't sent.
- **Name places with Apple Maps** is a button you can choose to press. It sends the coordinates of up to 40 of your most visited places to Apple to look up street names. Nothing happens unless you press it. Apple handles map requests under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

Throwback never reads your iPhone's own location. It only shows the history in the files you give it.

## Diagnostics log

Settings → Developer keeps a log on your iPhone to help fix problems: what you did in a session (next, back, pause, like…) and problems the app noticed (a video that didn't show, a clip that couldn't play). It identifies clips by short IDs and file names. It never contains names, chat titles, the content of chats, or coordinates (only counts, such as how many location points were read). It leaves your iPhone only if you share it, for example with **Report a problem**.

## Other people's content

Your export includes photos and videos your friends sent in chats. Throwback keeps them on your iPhone only. Please be thoughtful about what you share.

## Deleting your data

Settings → Delete everything removes all clips from Throwback. Deleting the app removes everything, including the diagnostics log. Your Snapchat export files aren't affected either way.

## Children

Throwback is meant for people old enough to use Snapchat (13 and over).

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.
